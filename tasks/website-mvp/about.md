# About Proposal

## Page Role

`/about/` is the concise public identity page for InKCre. Because the public project, product, and
organization use one name, it should explain them as one coherent effort rather than recreate the
legacy site's separate organization bureaucracy.

The page is website-owned public truth. It may use Hub product pressure as evidence, but it does
not require a mirrored internal “organization facts” package.

## Proposed Content Order

### 1. Identity

- Public name: `InKCre`
- Chinese name: `第三持存`
- One sentence identifying InKCre as the project and the open development effort around it.

Avoid `InKCreThing`, `翰墨化机团队`, a separate organization brand, or a legal-entity implication.

### 2. Why InKCre Exists

Condense the useful legacy origin into a durable problem:

- information is distributed across many systems;
- collecting it is not enough if it remains source-specific or difficult to retrieve;
- reusable information should remain available for later understanding, work, and creation.

Do not retain competitor comparisons, exhaustive personal feature wishes, “second brain”
positioning, or the complete founder story in the MVP.

### 3. Mission

Approved public wording:

> Help information remain reusable—from collection and organization to later use and creation.

The wording should connect the historical information-to-creation purpose to current product truth
without claiming that every stage of that vision is already delivered.

### 4. The Name

- `In` refers to Information.
- `K` refers to Knowledge.
- `Cre` refers to Creation.
- `InKCre` also evokes “ink”, “create”, and “increase”.
- State the Chinese name `第三持存` and its established English rendering, “tertiary retention”.
- Do not add a philosophical explanation in the MVP. The term is associated with Bernard
  Stiegler's account of memory exteriorized in technical supports; that evidence prevents the
  page from presenting the name as an invented brand etymology.

### 5. Current Stage

- InKCre is under active development.
- Product behavior, interfaces, and developer contracts may change.
- The website documents the current direction and the contracts that are real now.

This status is more useful than a timeline or stale roadmap.

### 6. Participate

- Link to the Developer Guide.
- Link to the InKCre GitHub organization.
- Invite code, documentation, design, and ecosystem participation without promising response time,
  acceptance, governance rights, or support channels that do not yet exist.

## Public Values

The MVP About page should state four confirmed public values:

- **Open source**: InKCre is developed in the open and should remain inspectable and contributable.
- **User control**: people should retain meaningful control over their information and product
  behavior.
- **Self-hosting**: running InKCre on infrastructure controlled by its users is a core direction.
- **Community-driven**: the product and its ecosystem should be shaped through open participation,
  not only by a closed central team.

These are values and development directions. The page must not turn them into claims that every
corresponding product capability, operational path, or governance mechanism is already complete.

## Explicitly Outside The MVP

- separate Team, Join, Mission, Origin, Governance, Funding, or Roadmap pages;
- maintainer cards and personal social links;
- legal entity, foundation, or community-governance claims;
- long competitor or product-comparison narratives;
- promises of self-hosting completeness, data control, platform support, or ecosystem stability;
- Chinese page publication before the `/zh/` locale has a complete maintained slice.

## Name Evidence

- Common English term: `tertiary retention`
- Associated philosopher: Bernard Stiegler
- Minimal MVP treatment: `InKCre is also known in Chinese as 第三持存 (tertiary retention).`
- No further philosophical exposition is required for the MVP.
- Primary English evidence: Bernard Stiegler describes tertiary retention as exteriorized or
  exosomatized memory in
  [The Neganthropocene](https://www.openhumanitiespress.org/books/download/Stiegler_2018_The-Neganthropocene.pdf).
- Chinese terminology evidence:
  [Nanjing University, “斯蒂格勒：政治问题亦是美学问题”](https://ptext.nju.edu.cn/info/1358/9343.htm)
  uses `第三持存` for `rétention tertiaire`.
