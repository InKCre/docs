# Business And Service Objectives

- business objective: help people put information to use by collecting it from the tools they already use, keeping it reusable beyond any one source or client, and making it available to people and trusted tools when needed.
- service objective: keep collection, info-base authority, organization, and application responsibilities distinct even when one service implements multiple parts.
- service objective: allow extensions to add capability without forking core ownership boundaries.
- success rationale: collection or storage alone does not realize the product's value; reusable information should remain available across runtimes and downstream workflows instead of being trapped in collection-specific flows.
